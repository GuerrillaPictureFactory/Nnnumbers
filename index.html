Numbers and players

Choose a regular name from the selector or choose “Type a new name…” to enter someone new. Tick Paid beside each player. Two players sharing a number split that number’s buy-in equally. Each M column is one match; tick the winning number.

Match payouts

The prize pool is split equally across matches by default. Choose a stage and enter an optional override to increase or change an individual match payout.
